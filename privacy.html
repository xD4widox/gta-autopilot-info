Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current personal-use version of GTA VI TikTok Autopilot. It must be reviewed and updated if the software or its data handling changes.

1. Operator

The application is operated by its developer for personal use. Privacy contact: dawidd2016@gmail.com.

2. What the desktop app may process

Depending on features enabled, the application may handle selected video sources, generated clips, draft captions, local job logs, account identifiers, and authorization credentials required to communicate with connected APIs. Details of collected data depend on the running build and enabled integrations.

3. Why this data is used

The purpose of processing is to authenticate authorized connections, discover content candidates, prepare media drafts, and perform any posting actions that the account owner has authorized and the provider permits.

4. Storage and sharing

The application is designed to run on the operator's computer. Local processing does not mean no information leaves the computer: enabling YouTube, TikTok, or other integrations can transmit data to those services under their separate policies. The operator should protect credentials and local files.

5. Website

This is a static informational website without its own visitor accounts, contact form, or custom analytics. The hosting provider may process technical connection information, such as IP addresses and access logs, under its own policies.

6. Retention and deletion

Local files, drafts, logs, and credentials can be removed by the operator through the relevant application or computer controls. Data held by external platforms is subject to those platforms' separate retention and deletion mechanisms. Actual retention settings should be checked against the current desktop application's implementation.

7. Security

API keys and access tokens must not be uploaded to this website or committed to its public repository. Authorization and security practices depend on the current desktop implementation and should be reviewed before connecting accounts.

8. Contact and updates

Questions can be sent to the contact address above. This policy may be updated when the project's functions or data practices change.
